# AgriMitra — AI-Powered Farming Assistant

AgriMitra is an AI-powered farming assistant designed to help farmers make informed agricultural decisions through crop guidance, weather information, and agricultural market price insights. The application aims to make farming-related information more accessible through a simple, farmer-friendly interface.

By bringing essential agricultural information together in one platform, AgriMitra helps farmers understand growing conditions, monitor weather, and explore market prices before making farming decisions.

## Overview

Farmers often need to consult multiple sources to understand crop requirements, weather conditions, and agricultural market prices. Finding reliable and timely information can be challenging, especially when digital services are difficult to navigate.

AgriMitra addresses this challenge by providing a unified platform for accessing agricultural information and AI-assisted guidance.

## Features

### Crop Guidance
- Access AI-assisted information about crop cultivation and farming practices.
- Explore guidance for common agricultural questions.
- Obtain information to support crop planning and management decisions.

### Weather Information
- Access weather information through an external weather service.
- Use available weather insights to support agricultural planning.
- Make more informed decisions about farming activities based on available conditions.

### Agricultural Market Prices
- Retrieve available commodity prices from agricultural market data sources.
- Explore reported mandi
